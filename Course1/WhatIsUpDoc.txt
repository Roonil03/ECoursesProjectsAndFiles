On the morning of my school presentation, I bit into an apple and felt something hard crack against my tooth. I had always avoided dentists because I disliked the sound of drilling and feared being told I had cavities.I ignored the pain and tried to continue practicing, but every word made the sharp edge of my broken tooth scrape my tongue. During my presentation, I suddenly tasted blood, stumbled over my speech, and felt the tooth crumble further in my mouth. My teacher called my mother, who took me straight to the dentist, where I finally learned that avoiding checkups was much more painful than attending them.
