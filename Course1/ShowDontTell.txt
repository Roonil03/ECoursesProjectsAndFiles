After years of searching, Vivian found the brass key in her mother's pocket the night she disappeared. It was in Dr. Sherlock's private collection. Vivian sneaked into the lab during a storm to steal the key out of the glass case. 

An alarm started blaring. 

Vivian turned to run but saw Dr. Sherlock pointing a gun at him. Just as he was about to shoot, a bottle from the nearby shelf fell off and broke. Vivian breathed in the silver mist and passed out. 

When he woke up in the hospital bed, the doctor informed him that he had caught a rare disease called Ashentold Fever. This would cause his lungs to fail within 24 hours. However, there was only one dose of the cure. Later that night, Vivian escaped the hospital and looked for Dr. Sherlock. Using the engravings on the brass key, he found himself at an old train station. At dawn, he found Dr. Sherlock standing next to a locked metal door. 

"I can spare your life if you give me the key," Sherlock said, holding up the cure. Vivian was shaking. If he gave him the key, he would never know why his mother had it. But if he didn't, then he would die. 

He looked at the key and saw it fit perfectly into the teeth of the lock. He put the key in and turned it. The door opened to reveal a little recording booth. The walls were full of pictures of his mother and many other missing people. All of a sudden, a hidden speaker sparked to life. "If you found this, Vivian, I am sorry I could not come home," his mother's voice whispered. 

The key had unlocked the truth. 

"Give me the antidote!" Vivian shouted. 

Sherlock refused. He tried to run, but Vivian broke the recording equipment, causing an emergency alert. The police arrived, and Sherlock dropped the cure while running away. Vivian picked it up and injected himself just in time before passing out. 

Vivian survived and kept the brass key as a symbol that he had finally found his mother.
