## 4. HighRoller Insights & Recommendations
- **Insight**: The decision tree strongly split users based entirely on `platformType`, heavily isolating `iphone` users as HighRollers and Android/PC users as PennyPinchers. 
- **Recommendation 1**: Redirect a larger percentage of ad-spend towards Apple iOS user acquisition, as they display significantly higher per-session monetization capability.
- **Recommendation 2**: Adjust the monetization strategy on Android and PC platforms. Since these users engage well but spend less on average, introduce highly appealing, low-cost transaction bundles (under $5.00) to monetize the larger volume of PennyPinchers.
